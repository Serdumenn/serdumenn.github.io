Pingi Pongi

Meet Pingi, the happiest ball you'll ever keep in the air. Slide one thumb to move your paddle, keep the rally going and chase a new record.

Free on Android. English, Türkçe, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Español, Português, Français.

Get it on Google Play

Features

Solo

Classic: one life, keep Pingi up as long as you can. Rush: 60 seconds, every hit counts.

One phone, many players

Table Duel for two, Co-op Rally as a team, and Party Table for up to four around a tablet.

Online

Portal Duel, Live Duel, Rush Battle for up to four players, and Online Co-op Rally with a friend anywhere.

Challenges

A Daily Challenge with the same serves for everyone, Ghost Challenges with a code, and world and friends rankings.

Friends

Add friends with a friend code, see who is online and invite them in one tap. No chat, and everyone gets a fun generated name.

Collection

Unlock Pingi faces, colourful paddles and themes. Sign in with Google Play Games to keep your progress on every phone.
